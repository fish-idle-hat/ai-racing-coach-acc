## Launch Post Draft

I just published AI Racing Coach - ACC, a local macOS prototype for Assetto Corsa Competizione coaching.

It captures ACC telemetry through a CrossOver helper, records sessions on macOS, gives live voice coaching, and generates replayable reports, reference-lap comparisons, and driver-profile insights.

The project is still early, and I am looking for partners to help improve the remaining mechanisms: better telemetry calibration, corner/apex detection, racing-line comparison, personalized coaching, setup-coach logic, and real-driver testing.
