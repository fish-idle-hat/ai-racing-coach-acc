# Partner Opportunities

ACC AI Coach is actively looking for partners who can help complete the remaining mechanisms and turn the prototype into a stronger training product.

## Current Focus

- Better telemetry calibration across tracks and cars.
- More accurate corner, apex, and racing-line comparison.
- Improved coaching logic for repeated habits and personalized learning plans.
- Setup-coach logic that separates driving-input problems from car-setup problems.
- UI polish, distribution, and real-user testing.

## Useful Partner Backgrounds

- Sim racing or ACC coaching experience.
- Telemetry analysis, motorsport data, or vehicle dynamics.
- macOS app development with SwiftUI.
- Windows/CrossOver interop or ACC shared-memory integration.
- AI product design, prompt engineering, or evaluation.

## Collaboration Note

The current app already runs locally as a prototype, but several mechanisms still need refinement before it should be treated as a finished commercial coach. If you are interested in improving the remaining systems, open an issue or contact the maintainer through GitHub.
